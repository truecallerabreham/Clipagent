# brag plan: ClipAgent

**What it is:** a `/screen-record` skill for Claude Code and Codex. You talk over your screen, and it hands back a studio-edited video with animations built from your own repo.
**Who it's for:** developers who want to make tutorials, walkthroughs and launch videos but hate editing.
**What sets it apart:** the animations come from *your* context (repo, docs, web) and land on the exact word you say. Five studio Looks. Everything runs locally.
**Most impressive claim:** "Talk freely. Ship studio quality." Mistakes are cut when you say "cut that".
**Visual hook:** a terminal where `/screen-record` types itself, then a red REC dot.
**Real UI and flow to show:** the actual command, the actual Architecture Flow render (JWT login with token pills), the real Look renders, the real deliverables list.
**Tone:** `polished` with punch: dark, confident, tight cuts on the beat.
**Share caption:** "Type /screen-record, talk, and ClipAgent ships the edited video: animations from your own code, synced to your words."

## Identity
- Background `#05060a`, the README banner gradient (cyan `#38BDF8` → purple `#A855F7` → amber `#F59E0B`).
- Inter 800 for headlines, JetBrains Mono for the terminal, Caveat Brush for marker accents (all bundled OFL fonts from the repo).

## Music
120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s), synthesized: a kick on every beat, offbeat hats, a sub bass following Am–F–C–G, a pluck arpeggio. Every scene cut falls on a bar line. SFX (a whoosh into each cut, a click on the REC dot, a tick on each Look card) sit on the same grid, mixed under the music.
