This week a small lab shipped a model called Nova Three, and the pricing is the real story.
Last year, a million output tokens on this tier cost thirty eight dollars.
Nova Three charges two dollars fifty. That is fifteen times cheaper.
Look at the leaderboard. Nova Three scores eighty nine point four on the reasoning suite, just behind the top model.
But the top model costs six times more per token.
So here is the trade off. Plot cost, accuracy and latency together, and the sweet spot is not the biggest model.
It is the mid size one, fast, cheap, and good enough for most agents.
If you build products on these models, re-run your evals this week.
